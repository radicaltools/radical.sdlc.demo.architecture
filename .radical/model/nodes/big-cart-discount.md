Carts from 200 get 5% off.

Answers from the brief's conversation:
1. Does the 5% stack with a discount code? Answer: b. No: a customer gets only the larger of the two.
2. Is the 200 measured before or after VAT? Answer: b. On the total with VAT, as the shipping threshold does.
3. Is the 200 measured before or after a discount code is taken off? Answer: a. Before any code, so a cart at 200 qualifies even with a code.
