# Estimated delivery date

A customer whose order has shipped wants to know when it will arrive, not only where it is.

The tracking of an order should also say the estimated delivery date, as a date like 2026-11-20. The carrier gives this date when the delivery is booked, and it stays as the carrier gave it.
