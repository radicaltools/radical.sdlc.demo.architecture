Customers fill a cart with coffee gear and see what it costs, VAT included. A welcome code and a loyalty code take a share off, but never more than a fixed amount. A customer places the order, pays, and we ship it. Until it is paid a customer may cancel it; once paid it can only be shipped. A cart must never come out at a negative price.
