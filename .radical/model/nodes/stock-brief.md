An order is paid only when its goods are there. Paying puts the goods aside in the warehouse; if they are short the customer is told which ones and the order is not paid. If the warehouse cannot be reached the payment is refused and the customer can try again. A payment repeated after a lost answer puts aside only once. Shipping a paid order means the warehouse books the courier, and the customer gets the tracking number. If the courier is down the order stays paid and can be shipped later.
