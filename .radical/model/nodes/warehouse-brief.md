The warehouse holds the goods the shop sells. When an order is paid the goods are put aside for it, all of it or nothing, so we never promise what we do not have. A shop that repeats a request because it lost the answer must not put aside twice. Goods not sent can be given back. Sending means booking the courier, and when the courier is down the goods stay put aside and we try later. Staff record what arrives. Anyone can ask how much of a product is available.
